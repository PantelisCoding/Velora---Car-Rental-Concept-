GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

A few helpful details before you start planning.

How do I request a vehicle? +

Pick a vehicle, choose your dates and complete the enquiry form. This demo prepares an email request; a confirmed reservation requires a response from the rental business.

Are the prices final? +

No. The displayed prices are example hourly rates. Final prices, taxes, fees and availability must be provided by the business.

What documents will I need? +

Rental requirements depend on the provider, vehicle and location. Typically a valid driving licence, identification and an accepted payment method are needed.

Can I change or cancel a booking? +

Changes and cancellations depend on the confirmed rental agreement. Ask for the exact terms before completing a reservation.
